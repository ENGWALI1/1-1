📘 PRESENT SIMPLE - زمن المضارع البسيط

مرحبا، أنا علي. في هذا الدرس، سنتعلم المضارع البسيط:
- متى نستخدمه؟
- كيف نكون الجملة؟
- الأخطاء الشائعة وتدريبات.

🎯 متى نستخدم المضارع البسيط؟

1. أشياء دائمة (Permanent)
   مثال: We live in New York.
         He works at a bank.

2. روتين (Routines)
   مثال: I wake up at 6:00 every day.

3. حقائق (Facts)
   مثال: The sun rises in the east.

4. جداول (Schedules)
   مثال: Our class starts at 9:00.

5. مع ظروف التكرار (adverbs of frequency)
   مثال: She always takes the bus.
         She never takes the bus.

🏗️ كيف نكون الجملة؟

✅ الإثبات (Positive):
   I / You / We / They + work
   He / She / It + works

❌ النفي (Negative):
   I don't work (do not)
   He doesn't work (does not)

❓ السؤال (Question):
   Do you work?
   Does he work?

⚠️ سؤال بكلمة استفهام:
   Where do you work?
   Where does he work?

✏️ التغييرات الإملائية مع He/She/It:

معظم الأفعال + s: work → works
تنتهي بـ sh,ch,ss,x,o + es: wash → washes
حرف ساكن + y → ies: study → studies
شواذ: go → goes, do → does, have → has

💬 الإجابات المختصرة:

Do they need help? → Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
Does he speak French? → Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
Do you like this song? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

⚠️ أخطاء شائعة:

❌ I am living in Tokyo. (إذا كان دائماً)
✅ I live in Tokyo.

❌ We likes to travel.
✅ We like to travel.

❌ They doesn't eat vegetables.
✅ They don't eat vegetables.

❌ Does you talk to him?
✅ Do you talk to him?

❌ She trys to save money.
✅ She tries to save money.

❌ What time you finish work?
✅ What time do you finish work?

📝 تدريبات:

1. I take → He takes
2. She does → We do
3. You enjoy → She enjoys
4. They study (نفي) → They don't study
5. He sings (نفي) → He doesn't sing
6. She wants to buy a phone (سؤال) → Does she want to buy a phone?
7. They sell books online (سؤال) → Do they sell books online?

✅ الخلاصة:
لإتقان المضارع البسيط، يجب أن تقدر تكون:
• They live in Amsterdam. (مثبت)
• They don't live in Amsterdam. (منفي)
• Do they live in Amsterdam? (سؤال)

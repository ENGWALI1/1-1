📘 *Will vs Going To — الفرق بين Will و Going To*
✨ *شرح كامل ومفصل 100%*

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🎯 *أولاً: الفرق الأساسي*

كلاهما يستخدمان للتحدث عن المستقبل، لكن لهما معاني مختلفة:

🔹 *Will*
• قرار في لحظة الكلام *(unplanned)*
• غير رسمي *(يمكن استخدامه في الرسمي)*
• غير مؤكد *(uncertain)*
• ممكن *(possible)*

🔹 *Going To*
• خطة مسبقة / قرار مدروس *(planned)*
• غير رسمي فقط
• مؤكد *(certain)*
• قرار متخذ بالفعل *(decided)*

📌 *المقارنة:*

✅ I'm going to call Jim at 3:00.
(خططت لها - عندي موعد)

✅ I'll call him in a few minutes.
(قررت الآن - لم تكن في خطتي)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🟢 *ثانياً: متى نستخدم كل واحد؟*

📌 *استخدامات Going To*

1️⃣ *خطط مسبقة (Plans)*
✅ I'm going to study medicine.
(قررت من قبل)

━━━━━━━━

2️⃣ *شيء مؤكد الحدوث (Certain)*
✅ We're going to visit Spain next month.
(حجزنا التذاكر)

━━━━━━━━

3️⃣ *شيء مقرر مسبقاً (Already decided)*
✅ I'm going to have lunch at noon.
(في جدول أعمالي)

━━━━━━━━

4️⃣ *دليل واضح على حدوث شيء (Evidence)*
✅ Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📌 *استخدامات Will*

1️⃣ *قرار في لحظة الكلام (Unplanned)*
✅ "Jim called you."
→ "Ok, I'll call him back."

━━━━━━━━

2️⃣ *تنبؤات غير مؤكدة (Uncertain predictions)*
✅ We'll probably visit Spain next month.
(لسنا متأكدين)

━━━━━━━━

3️⃣ *وعود وعروض (Promises & Offers)*
✅ I'll help you with your homework.
✅ I'll never lie to you again.

━━━━━━━━

4️⃣ *مواقف رسمية (Formal situations)*
✅ Ladies and gentlemen, we will begin the conference at 9:00.

━━━━━━━━

5️⃣ *التعبير عن الاعتقاد (Belief)*
✅ I believe he'll pass the test.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🏗️ *ثالثاً: كيف نكون الجملة؟*

📌 *Future Simple (Will)*

✅ *المثبت:*
I will work.
You will work.
He will work.
We will work.
They will work.

❌ *المنفي:*
I will not work.
(I won't work)

❓ *السؤال:*
Will you work?
Will he work?

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📌 *Going To*

✅ *المثبت:*
I am going to work.
You are going to work.
He is going to work.
We are going to work.
They are going to work.

❌ *المنفي:*
I am not going to work.
(I'm not going to work)

You are not going to work.
(You aren't going to work)

❓ *السؤال:*
Are you going to work?
Is he going to work?

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💬 *رابعاً: الاختصارات (Contractions)*

📌 *Will*

I will → I'll
You will → You'll
He will → He'll
She will → She'll
It will → It'll
We will → We'll
They will → They'll

━━━━━━━━

📌 *Going To*

I am going to → I'm going to
You are going to → You're going to
He is going to → He's going to
She is going to → She's going to
It is going to → It's going to
We are going to → We're going to
They are going to → They're going to

⚠️ *النفي في Going To:*

• I'm not going to work
• You're not going to work
• You aren't going to work
• He's not going to work
• He isn't going to work

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📊 *خامساً: جدول المقارنة التفصيلي*

🔹 قرار في لحظة الكلام
❌ Going To
✅ Will → I'll get it

━━━━━━━━

🔹 خطة مسبقة
✅ I'm going to study
❌ Will

━━━━━━━━

🔹 تنبؤ مؤكد *(مع دليل)*
✅ Look at the clouds!
❌ Will

━━━━━━━━

🔹 تنبؤ غير مؤكد *(مع maybe)*
❌ Going To
✅ It'll probably rain

━━━━━━━━

🔹 وعود
❌ Going To
✅ I'll help you

━━━━━━━━

🔹 موقف رسمي
❌ Going To
✅ We will begin at 9:00

━━━━━━━━

🔹 شيء مقرر مسبقاً
✅ I'm going to call at 3:00
❌ Will

━━━━━━━━

🔹 شيء مؤكد الحدوث
✅ We're going to Spain
❌ Will

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔄 *سادساً: متى يمكن استخدام الاثنين معاً؟*

في بعض الجمل يمكن استخدام كليهما، خاصة في التنبؤات *(predictions)*

📌 مثال:

✅ The weather channel says it's going to snow tomorrow.

✅ The weather channel says it'll snow tomorrow.

📌 في نفس الجملة:

"I'm going to study for my exam and then maybe I'll watch a show."

(أكيد: going to → دراسة / غير أكيد: will → مشاهدة)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

❌ *سابعاً: أخطاء شائعة وتصحيحها*

1️⃣ استخدام going to في القرارات الفورية:

❌ Wait, I'm going to get it!
✅ Wait, I'll get it!

━━━━━━━━

2️⃣ استخدام will في الخطط المسبقة:

❌ I will call Jim at 3:00
*(إذا كانت خطة مسجلة)*

✅ I'm going to call Jim at 3:00.

━━━━━━━━

3️⃣ نسيان الفعل المساعد في going to:

❌ She going to travel tomorrow.
✅ She is going to travel tomorrow.

━━━━━━━━

4️⃣ خطأ في الاختصار:

❌ They'll not come. *(قليل الاستخدام)*
✅ They won't come.

━━━━━━━━

5️⃣ استخدام will مع maybe بطريقة خاطئة:

❌ Maybe it's going to rain.
*(صحيحة لكن الأفضل will)*

✅ Maybe it'll rain.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📝 *ثامناً: كلمات مفتاحية لكل زمن*

📌 *Going To (خطط مسبقة)*

• tonight
• tomorrow
• next week
• this weekend
• on Monday

• in the morning

⚠️ *(عندما يكون هناك توقيت محدد)*

━━━━━━━━

📌 *Will (لحظي / وعود / تنبؤات)*

• probably
• maybe
• I think
• I hope
• I promise
• I'm sure
• I believe
• definitely
• certainly

⚠️ *(عندما يكون غير مؤكد)*

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📝 *تاسعاً: تمارين تدريبية*

📌 *التمرين 1: اختر Going To أو Will*

1. Look at those clouds! It (is going to / will) rain.

2. A: The phone is ringing.
   B: I (am going to / will) get it.

3. We (are going to / will) visit Paris next summer.
   We already booked the tickets.

4. I think she (is going to / will) pass the exam.

5. They (are going to / will) get married next month.
   They sent the invitations.

6. Maybe I (am going to / will) see you later.

7. I promise I (am going to / will) never be late again.

8. What (are you going to / will you) do after graduation?
   *(خطط)*

━━━━━━━━

📌 *التمرين 2: أكمل الجمل بالشكل الصحيح*

1. I ________ (call) you when I get home. *(وعد)*

2. She ________ (study) law at university. *(خطة مسبقة)*

3. They ________ (probably / arrive) late.

4. He ________ (travel) to Egypt next week. *(حجز التذاكر)*

5. Wait! I ________ (help) you with that bag.

━━━━━━━━

📌 *التمرين 3: صحح الخطأ في الجمل التالية*

1. She going to start a new job tomorrow.

2. I think it's going to snow, but I'm not sure.

3. They'll not come to the party. *(صحيح؟)*

4. I promise I'm going to help you.

5. Look at the time! We'll be late for sure.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✅ *عاشراً: الخلاصة النهائية*

🔑 Going To
= خطط مسبقة، قرار مدروس، مؤكد

🔑 Will
= قرار لحظي، وعود، تنبؤات غير مؤكدة، مواقف رسمية

🔑 في التنبؤات، يمكن استخدام الاثنين *(خاصة مع دليل)*

🔑 Going To تحتاج:
`am / is / are + going to + verb`

🔑 Will تحتاج:
`will + verb`

🔑 اختصار will not
= won't

🔑 اختصار going to:

I'm going to
You're going to
He's going to

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💡 *الجمل التي تخلّصلك كل شيء:*

✅ I'm going to study medicine.
(خطة مسبقة - مؤكد)

✅ I'll answer the phone.
(قرار لحظي - لم أخطط له)

━━━━━━━━

✅ We're going to Spain next month.
(مؤكد - حجزنا)

✅ We'll probably go to Spain next month.
(غير مؤكد)

━━━━━━━━

✅ I promise I'll help you.
(وعد → will)

✅ Look at the clouds! It's going to rain.
(دليل → going to)

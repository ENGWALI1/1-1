📘 الفرق بين Present Simple و Present Continuous
شرح مقارن كامل

🎯 أولاً: الفرق الأساسي

Present Simple (I work):
يدل على شيء صحيح بشكل عام أو شيء دائم أو شيء يستمر لفترة طويلة

Present Continuous (I am working):
يدل على شيء يحدث الآن أو شيء مؤقت أو شيء يحدث لفترة قصيرة

مقارنة:
✅ I live in this building. (مكان إقامتي الدائم)
✅ I am staying at a hotel. (مؤقت - فقط لبعض الوقت)

✅ He plays the guitar. (يعرف يعزف - قدرة عامة)
✅ He is playing the guitar. (يعزف الآن)

✅ We watch the news at 9pm. (عادة يومية - روتين)
✅ We are watching the news. (يحدث الآن)

🟢 ثانياً: جدول المقارنة الكامل

دائم / مؤقت:
Present Simple: دائم (permanent) - I live here.
Present Continuous: مؤقت (temporary) - I am staying here.

الآن / ليس الآن:
Present Simple: ليس الآن (في العادة) - He plays guitar.
Present Continuous: الآن (right now) - He is playing now.

حقائق علمية (Facts):
Present Simple: ✅ The sun sets in the west (حقيقة ثابتة)
Present Continuous: ❌

روتين (Routine):
Present Simple: ✅ We watch news at 9pm
Present Continuous: ❌

تغيرات وتطورات (Trends):
Present Simple: ❌
Present Continuous: ✅ The price is rising (السعر في ارتفاع)

عادة إيجابية (Good habits):
Present Simple: ✅ She always takes the bus
Present Continuous: ❌

عادة سلبية (Complaint):
Present Simple: ❌
Present Continuous: ✅ She is always complaining (دايماً تتذمر - شكوى)

جداول مواعيد (Schedules):
Present Simple: ✅ The flight leaves at 7pm (رحلة منتظمة)
Present Continuous: ❌

يحدث الآن:
Present Simple: ❌
Present Continuous: ✅ The flight is leaving (الرحلة تغادر الآن)

🟡 ثالثاً: كيف نكون الجملة؟

Present Simple:
✅ مثبت: I work / You work / He works / She works
❌ منفي: I do not work / He does not work
❓ سؤال: Do you work? / Does he work?

Present Continuous:
✅ مثبت: I am working / He is working / They are working
❌ منفي: I am not working / He is not working
❓ سؤال: Are you working? / Is he working?

⚠️ رابعاً: الفرق الأهم - Stative Verbs (أفعال الحالة)

معظم الأفعال العادية (Action Verbs): نستخدمها مع الزمنين ✅
مثل: run, jump, read, write, sing, dance

أفعال الحالة (Stative Verbs): ✅ تستخدم مع Present Simple ❌ لا تستخدم مع Present Continuous

أهم أفعال الحالة التي يجب حفظها:

المشاعر: like, love, hate, prefer, enjoy
الرغبات: want, need, wish, desire
الإدراك: know, understand, realize, recognize
الحواس: see, hear, smell, taste, feel
الملكية: have (بمعنى يملك), own, belong, possess
الرأي: believe, think (بمعنى أعتقد), agree
القياس: weigh, measure, cost, seem, appear

أمثلة:
❌ I am liking this movie. → ✅ I like this movie.
❌ Are you understanding? → ✅ Do you understand?
❌ I am needing help. → ✅ I need help.
❌ He is having a car. → ✅ He has a car.
❌ She is knowing the answer. → ✅ She knows the answer.

🔄 خامساً: أمثلة مقارنة مباشرة

• Present Simple: I live in Damascus. (دائم)
• Present Continuous: I am living with my friend this month. (مؤقت)

• Present Simple: She works at a bank. (وظيفة دائمة)
• Present Continuous: She is working on a new project. (مؤقت)

• Present Simple: The sun rises in the east. (حقيقة علمية)
• Present Continuous: Look! The sun is rising! (يحدث الآن)

• Present Simple: I drink tea every morning. (عادة يومية)
• Present Continuous: I am drinking tea now. (الآن)

• Present Simple: The train leaves at 8am. (جدول مواعيد)
• Present Continuous: The train is leaving! (تغادر الآن)

🚨 سادساً: أخطاء شائعة وتصحيحها

1. ❌ Look! It snows! → ✅ Look! It is snowing! (لأن Look! تدل على شيء يحدث الآن)

2. ❌ She works in the Tokyo office this month. → ✅ She is working in the Tokyo office this month. (this month = فترة مؤقتة)

3. ❌ We are making websites. (كرد على "ماذا تعمل الشركة؟") → ✅ We make websites. (عمل الشركة الدائم)

4. ❌ He gives a presentation now. → ✅ He is giving a presentation now. (now = present continuous)

5. ❌ Unemployment goes down this year. → ✅ Unemployment is going down this year. (this year = trend)

6. ❌ They're always going to bed at 11:00. (عادة يومية) → ✅ They always go to bed at 11:00.

7. ❌ Are you liking chocolate cake? → ✅ Do you like chocolate cake? (like = stative verb)

8. ❌ What are you doing? (في مقابلة عمل - تقصد الوظيفة) → ✅ What do you do?

9. ❌ She is living in Brazil. (بدون سياق أنها مؤقتة) → ✅ She lives in Brazil. (دائم)

📝 سابعاً: تمارين تطبيقية

التمرين 1: أكمل بالزمن الصحيح

1. Usually, I drink (drink) tea, but today I am drinking (drink) coffee.
2. Usually, he starts (start) work at 8:00, but today he is starting (start) at 9:00.
3. Usually, we listen (listen) to rock music, but today we are listening (listen) to jazz.
4. Usually, they make (make) rice, but today they are making (make) noodles.

التمرين 2: اختر الزمن الصحيح

1. Look! It (is snowing).
2. She (is working) in the Tokyo office this month.
3. What (do you do) for a living?
4. The price (rises) every year.
5. The price (is rising) this year.
6. He (is playing) the guitar right now.
7. She (always takes) the bus to work.
8. She (is always complaining) about the bus. (شكوى)

التمرين 3: صحح الخطأ في الجمل التالية

1. We are making websites for a living. → We make websites for a living.
2. He gives a presentation now. → He is giving a presentation now.
3. Are you wanting to come with us? → Do you want to come with us?
4. I am living in Paris since 2010. → I have been living in Paris since 2010.
5. What are you doing? (في مقابلة عمل) → What do you do?

✅ ثامناً: الخلاصة النهائية

Present Simple:
🕐 دائم، عادة، روتين، حقيقة
📝 I/You/We/They + verb
📝 He/She/It + verb+s/es
❌ don't / doesn't + verb
❓ Do/Does + subject + verb?
✅ كل الأفعال (بما فيها stative)

Present Continuous:
🕐 الآن، مؤقت، متغير
📝 am/is/are + verb + ing
❌ am not/isn't/aren't
❓ Am/Is/Are + subject?
❌ Stative verbs

💡 الجمل التي تخلّصلك كل شيء:

• I live in Damascus. (دائم - Simple)
• I am staying at a hotel this week. (مؤقت - Continuous)

• The sun sets in the west. (حقيقة - Simple)
• Look! The sun is setting. (الآن - Continuous)

• She always takes the bus. (عادة - Simple)
• She is always complaining. (شكوى - Continuous)

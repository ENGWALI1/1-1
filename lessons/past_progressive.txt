🎯 أولاً: ما هو Past Continuous؟

الماضي المستمر (Past Continuous / Past Progressive) هو زمن يستخدم لوصف:
- حدث كان **مستمراً** خلال **فترة زمنية** في الماضي
- حدث كان مستمراً في **لحظة محددة** في الماضي
- حدث مستمر **قاطعه حدث آخر**

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     Timeline                                   │
│                                                                 │
│   9:00am ───────────────────► 5:00pm                           │
│            I was working                                       │
│                                                                 │
│    Past Simple (نقطة)          Past Continuous (فترة)         │
│    I started at 9:00am          I was working from 9am to 5pm  │
│    I finished at 5:00pm                                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🟢 ثانياً: متى نستخدم Past Continuous؟

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1️⃣ حدث مستمر خلال فترة زمنية                                    │
│    Sarah **was studying** all day.                             │
│    (كانت سارة تدرس طوال اليوم)                                 │
│                                                                 │
│ 2️⃣ حدث مستمر في لحظة محددة في الماضي                           │
│    At 11:00pm, I **was sleeping**.                             │
│    (الساعة 11 مساءً، كنت نائماً)                               │
│                                                                 │
│ 3️⃣ حدث مستمر قاطعه حدث آخر                                     │
│    They **were having** dinner **when** we arrived.            │
│    (كانوا يتناولون العشاء عندما وصلنا)                         │
│                                                                 │
│ 4️⃣ خطة أو فكرة في الماضي (لم تتحقق)                           │
│    I **was planning** to call John, but I didn't have time.    │
│    (كنت أخطط للاتصال بجون، لكن لم يكن لدي وقت)                  │
│                                                                 │
│ 5️⃣ طلب مهذب (مع I was wondering...)                           │
│    I **was wondering** if you could help me.                   │
│    (كنت أتساءل إذا كان بإمكانك مساعدتي)                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🏗️ ثالثاً: تكوين الجملة (Formation)

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ✅ المثبت (Positive)                                           │
│ I was working.    He was working.    She was working.          │
│ It was working.   You were working.  We were working.          │
│ They were working.                                             │
│                                                                 │
│ ❌ المنفي (Negative)                                            │
│ I was not working (I wasn't working)                           │
│ You were not working (You weren't working)                     │
│ He was not working (He wasn't working)                         │
│ We were not working (We weren't working)                       │
│ They were not working (They weren't working)                   │
│                                                                 │
│ ❓ السؤال (Question)                                            │
│ Was I working?   Were you working?   Was he working?           │
│ Was she working? Was it working?    Were we working?           │
│ Were they working?                                             │
│                                                                 │
│ ❓ سؤال بكلمة استفهام:                                         │
│ Where **was he working**? / Why **were they laughing**?         │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

⚠️ رابعاً: الاختصارات (Contractions)

في الماضي المستمر، **لا نستخدم اختصارات في الجمل المثبتة**، ولكن في الجمل المنفية نستخدم:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ كاملة              │ مختصرة        │ النطق                      │
├────────────────────┼───────────────┼────────────────────────────┤
│ I was not          │ I wasn't      │ آي وازنت                  │
│ You were not       │ You weren't   │ يو ورنت                   │
│ He was not         │ He wasn't     │ هي وازنت                  │
│ She was not        │ She wasn't    │ شي وازنت                  │
│ It was not         │ It wasn't     │ إت وازنت                  │
│ We were not        │ We weren't    │ وي ورنت                   │
│ They were not      │ They weren't  │ داي ورنت                  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

⚠️ **ملاحظة**: يجب نطق الـ "t" في نهاية الكلمة، وإلا ستفقد المعنى!

✏️ خامساً: التغييرات الإملائية (Spelling Rules)

قبل إضافة -ing:

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ القاعدة                    │ Base Form │ Verb + ing            │
├────────────────────────────┼───────────┼───────────────────────┤
│ 1️⃣ معظم الأفعال + ing      │ talk      │ talking                │
│                            │ sell      │ selling                │
├────────────────────────────┼───────────┼───────────────────────┤
│ 2️⃣ تنتهي بـ e → نحذف e + ing│ make      │ making                 │
│                            │ take      │ taking                 │
├────────────────────────────┼───────────┼───────────────────────┤
│ 3️⃣ تنتهي بـ ie → ying       │ tie       │ tying                  │
│                            │ lie       │ lying                  │
├────────────────────────────┼───────────┼───────────────────────┤
│ 4️⃣ C+V+C (ضعف الحرف        │ jog       │ jogging                │
│    الأخير) + ing           │ sit       │ sitting                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

⚠️ سادساً: متى لا نستخدم Past Continuous؟

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ لا نستخدم Past Continuous مع **Stative Verbs** (أفعال الحالة)  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

❌ I **was knowing** him for many years.
✅ I **knew** him for many years.

❌ Susan **was hating** spiders.
✅ Susan **hated** spiders.

❌ They **were owning** a big house.
✅ They **owned** a big house.

❌ We **were wanting** to call you.
✅ We **wanted** to call you.

❌ She **was needing** to catch her flight.
✅ She **needed** to catch her flight.

📌 **استثناء**: الفعل "have" يمكن استخدامه في المستمر إذا كان معناه "يقيم حفلة" أو "يمتع":
✅ He **was having** a party. (يُقيم حفلة)
✅ He **was having** a good time. (يمتع وقته)
❌ He **was having** a computer. (يمتلك - غلط)

📝 سابعاً: الإجابات المختصرة (Short Answers)

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ السؤال                               │ ✅ إيجابي    │ ❌ سلبي    │
├──────────────────────────────────────┼─────────────┼────────────┤
│ Was he playing video games?          │ Yes, he was.│ No, he     │
│                                      │             │ wasn't.    │
│ Were they studying for the test?     │ Yes, they   │ No, they   │
│                                      │ were.       │ weren't.   │
│ Were you managing a team?            │ Yes, I was. │ No, I      │
│                                      │             │ wasn't.    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

⚠️ **قاعدة**: في الإجابة الإيجابية المختصرة، **لا نستخدم الاختصار** (لا نقول Yes, he was)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

❌ ثامناً: أخطاء شائعة وتصحيحها

1️⃣ **استخدام زمن خاطئ (حاضر بدل ماضي)**:
❌ I **am studying** when you called.
✅ I **was studying** when you called.

2️⃣ **خطأ في فعل to be**:
❌ We **was** relaxing.
✅ We **were** relaxing.

3️⃣ **خطأ إملائي (نسيان حذف e)**:
❌ She was **bakeing** a cake.
✅ She was **baking** a cake.

4️⃣ **ترتيب كلمات خاطئ في السؤال**:
❌ When you were going to call?
✅ **When were you going** to call?

5️⃣ **نسيان فعل to be**:
❌ You waiting a long time?
✅ **Were you** waiting a long time?

❌ He talking to a client.
✅ He **was** talking to a client.

6️⃣ **استخدام Stative Verbs**:
❌ I **was believing** you.
✅ I **believed** you.

📝 تاسعاً: تمارين تدريبية

**التمرين 1: حوّل إلى Past Continuous (Positive)**

1. I am learning. → I ______ learning.
2. We are waiting. → We ______ waiting.
3. He is singing. → He ______ singing.

**التمرين 2: حوّل إلى Past Continuous (Negative)**

1. Ruby was teaching. → Ruby ______ teaching.
2. Simon was winning. → Simon ______ winning. (باستخدام اختصار)
3. We were driving. → We ______ driving. (باستخدام اختصار)

**التمرين 3: حوّل إلى سؤال**

1. Steven was going to the conference. (Where) → Where ______ Steven ______?
2. She was doing her homework. (What) → What ______ she ______?

**التمرين 4: صحح الخطأ في الجمل التالية**

1. We was relaxing.
2. She was bakeing a cake.
3. You waiting a long time?
4. He talking to a client.
5. I was believing you.

💡 الجمل التي تخلّصلك كل شيء:

• I **was working** all day yesterday. (حدث مستمر)
• At 8pm, I **was watching** TV. (لحظة محددة)
• They **were having** dinner **when** I arrived. (مقاطعة)

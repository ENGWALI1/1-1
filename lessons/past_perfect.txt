📘 Past Perfect Tense - الماضي التام
شرح كامل ومفصل 100%

🎯 أولاً: ما هو الماضي التام؟

الماضي التام يُسمى أحياناً "ماضي الماضي" (the past of the past).

الفكرة الأساسية: عندما نتحدث عن حدثين أو أكثر وقعوا في الماضي، ونريد أن نُظهر أيّهم حدث **أولاً**، نستخدم الماضي التام للحدث الذي وقع أولاً.

المقارنة:

جملتين منفصلتين (Past Simple):           جملة واحدة (مع Past Perfect):
The meeting started at 9:00.             When we arrived at 10:00, the meeting **had started**.
We arrived at 10:00.                     (عندما وصلنا الساعة 10، كان الاجتماع قد بدأ)

الحدث الأول (9:00) → Past Perfect (had started)
الحدث الثاني (10:00) → Past Simple (arrived)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🟢 ثانياً: متى نستخدم الماضي التام؟

1️⃣ للتعبير عن حدثين في الماضي، نُظهر أيّهم حدث أولاً

Before the guests arrived, we **had cleaned** the house.
(قبل وصول الضيوف، كنا قد نظفنا المنزل)

When the president came to power, the economy **had improved**.
(عندما وصل الرئيس إلى السلطة، كان الاقتصاد قد تحسن)

2️⃣ في جمل تحتوي على كلمات مثل: before, after, by the time, when

By the time Maria moved to Canada, she **had graduated** from university.
(بحلول الوقت الذي انتقلت فيه ماريا إلى كندا، كانت قد تخرجت من الجامعة)

3️⃣ عندما يكون الحدث الأول مفهوماً من السياق

Someone asked: "What was the situation when the president came to power?"
You answer: "The economy **had improved**, the currency **had strengthened**..."

🏗️ ثالثاً: كيف نكون الجملة؟

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              الصيغة العامة                                      │
│           الفاعل + had + past participle (V3)                   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

✅ المثبت (Positive)

I had worked.    You had worked.    We had worked.
They had worked. He had worked.      She had worked.
It had worked.

❌ المنفي (Negative)

I had not worked.    You had not worked.    He had not worked.
She had not worked.  We had not worked.     They had not worked.

الاختصارات:
I hadn't worked, You hadn't worked, He hadn't worked...

❓ السؤال (Question)

Had I worked?    Had you worked?    Had he worked?
Had she worked?  Had we worked?     Had they worked?

❓ سؤال بكلمة استفهام:
Where **had** you worked before you joined this company?
Who **had** you worked with?

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚠️ رابعاً: حالة خاصة - الفعل "to have" نفسه

عندما يكون الفعل الأساسي هو "have"، نصبح عندنا "had had":

I **had had** breakfast before I left home.
(كنت قد تناولت الفطور قبل أن أغادر المنزل)

نفي: I hadn't had breakfast when I left home.
سؤال: Had you had breakfast when you left home?

🔤 سادساً: الفرق بين for و since

for + مدة زمنية (period of time):
- for five years, for two hours, for a long time, for ages

since + نقطة بداية (point in time):
- since 2018, since January, since I was a child

💬 سابعاً: الاختصارات (Contractions)

المثبت:
I had  → I'd      You had → You'd    We had → We'd
They had → They'd  He had → He'd      She had → She'd

⚠️ انتبه: I'd يمكن أن تعني:
- I had (في Past Perfect) → I'd worked.
- I would (في Conditional) → I'd love to help.

الطريقة للتفريق: من الكلمة التي تليها
- I'd + past participle (worked, gone, seen) → I had
- I'd + مصدر الفعل (work, go, see) → I would

النفي:
I had not → I hadn't    You had not → You hadn't
He had not → He hadn't   She had not → She hadn't
We had not → We hadn't   They had not → They hadn't

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💬 ثامناً: الإجابات المختصرة (Short Answers)

السؤال:                      ✅ إيجابي:        ❌ سلبي:
Had she finished the report?  Yes, she had.    No, she hadn't.
Had they informed their clients? Yes, they had. No, they hadn't.
Had it happened before?       Yes, it had.     No, it hadn't.

🔔 قاعدة مهمة:
- في الإجابة الإيجابية، لا نستخدم الاختصار ❌ (لا نقول Yes, she'd)
- في الإجابة السلبية، نستخدم الاختصار ✅ (No, she hadn't)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✏️ تاسعاً: الأفعال المنتظمة (Regular Verbs)

مع الأفعال المنتظمة، past participle = الفعل + ed

│ القاعدة                     │ مثال                           │
├─────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────┤
│ معظم الأفعال + ed           │ talk → talked, watch → watched │
│ تنتهي بـ e + d              │ live → lived, love → loved     │
│ حرف ساكن + y → ied          │ cry → cried, study → studied   │
│ C+V+C (ضعف الحرف الأخير)   │ stop → stopped, plan → planned │

❌ حادي عشر: أخطاء شائعة وتصحيحها

1️⃣ استخدام past simple بدل past participle:
❌ We had went to the mall.
✅ We had gone to the mall.

2️⃣ خطأ إملائي (نسيان مضاعفة الحرف):
❌ Who had planed the party?
✅ Who had planned the party?

3️⃣ خطأ في اختصار النفي:
❌ They had'nt been there before.
✅ They hadn't been there before.

4️⃣ إجابة مختصرة خاطئة:
❌ Had you seen that movie before? → Yes, I seen.
✅ Had you seen that movie before? → Yes, I had.

5️⃣ إجابة مختصرة باستخدام زمن خاطئ:
❌ Had you seen that movie before? → No, I didn't.
✅ Had you seen that movie before? → No, I hadn't.

6️⃣ عدم استخدام past perfect عندما يجب:
❌ When we arrived, he went out already.
✅ When we arrived, he had already gone out.

7️⃣ نسيان had:
❌ She been to Paris before.
✅ She had been to Paris before.

📝 ثالث عشر: تمارين تدريبية

التمرين 1: حوّل الجمل إلى Past Perfect

1. Before the plane took off, the passengers (find) their seats.
2. By the time we got home, the ice cream (melt).
3. It (stop) snowing before we landed.
4. Before the babysitter arrived, the kids (go) to bed.

التمرين 2: أكمل بالزمن الصحيح (Past Simple أو Past Perfect)

1. When I arrived at the station, the train ______ (already / leave).
2. She ______ (never / see) such a beautiful sunset before.
3. They ______ (finish) their homework before they went out.
4. He ______ (live) in London for five years before he moved to Paris.
5. By the time we reached the cinema, the movie ______ (start).

التمرين 3: صحح الخطأ في الجمل التالية

1. When we arrived, he went out already.
2. She had went to the supermarket.
3. Had you ever been to London before? → Yes, I'd.
4. They hadn't never seen snow.
5. I had be there before.

• When we arrived, the meeting **had already started**.
  (عندما وصلنا، كان الاجتماع قد بدأ بالفعل)

• She **had never seen** snow before she visited Canada.
  (لم تكن قد رأت الثلج من قبل قبل أن تزور كندا)

• By the time I woke up, my brother **had already left**.
  (بحلول الوقت الذي استيقظت فيه، كان أخي قد غادر بالفعل)

  (كنت قد تناولت الفطور قبل أن أذهب إلى العمل)
